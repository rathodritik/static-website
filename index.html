01 — Materials

Three grades. One standard.

Every cover starts life as a flat hide or bolt of technical cloth, cut against a digitized pattern of your seat — not a universal template stretched to fit.

Full-Grain Hide

MATERIAL / FG-40

Vegetable-tanned leather, aniline-finished so the grain stays visible. Softens and patinas with use, the way a good boot does.

Quilted Alcantara

MATERIAL / QA-12

Diamond-stitched microfiber suede over 5mm foam backing. Cooler in summer, warmer in winter, near-silent against fabric.

Technical Weave

MATERIAL / TW-08

Waterproof ballistic weave built for kids, dogs, and job sites. Wipes clean, resists tearing, still tailored to the seam.

02 — Detail

Built to disappear into your car, not sit on top of it.

A

Airbag-certified seams

Break-stitched over side and curtain airbag lines so deployment isn't obstructed — tested to OEM spec, not just claimed.

B

Console & buckle cutouts

Every lever, latch, and headrest post is mapped from your seat's own geometry before a single panel is cut.

C

Custom stitch colorway

Fourteen thread colors, from bone to burgundy, so the seam matches your interior instead of fighting it.

D

No-tool install

Elastic hooks and factory anchor points only. Most drivers finish both front seats in under 25 minutes.

03 — Fitment

Tell us the car. We already have the pattern.

Check my pattern

Every pattern in our library is cut from a physical seat, not a photo. If your exact trim isn't yet on file, our pattern team digitizes it from three reference photos you send us — no shop visit required.

Front bucket seats, rear bench, and captain's chairs are patterned separately, so a two-row SUV gets three fitted sets, not one stretched sheet.
